# Privacy Policy for Orbit

**Last updated:** 2026-07-27

This policy covers the Android app **Orbit** (package id `com.fazeel.orbit`),
a single-player, offline puzzle game.

## Summary

Orbit does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data,
because it does not connect to the internet at all. There are no accounts,
no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases in this version of the app.

## What the app does locally

- Orbit keeps a single local save file on your device (your high score and
  a mute/settings preference). This file never leaves your device, is never
  transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
- Orbit does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos,
  microphone, camera, or any other personal information.
- Orbit does not use any third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics,
  crash reporting, or social features.

## Permissions

Orbit requests only the Android `VIBRATE` permission, used to produce short
haptic feedback when orbs merge, when the top tier clears, and on game
over. This permission does not grant access to any personal data and is not
used for any purpose other than triggering the device's vibration motor.

Orbit does **not** request the `INTERNET` permission and cannot make network
requests.

## Data collection, sharing, and sale

- **Data collected:** None.
- **Data shared with third parties:** None (there are no third parties
  integrated into the app).
- **Data sold:** None.
- **Data transmitted off the device:** None — the app has no network
  permission and makes no network calls.

## Children's privacy

Orbit does not collect any data from any user, including children, and
contains no ads, in-app purchases, chat, or other online features, so there
is no online-safety exposure from using the app.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Orbit adds any data collection, advertising,
analytics, or online features, this policy will be updated *before* that
version is released, and the in-app and Play Store declarations will be
updated to match. The current (v1) version of Orbit ships with none of the
above.
